Streamnest uses Google sign-in to identify your account. We store your Google account identifier, email address, display name, profile image URL, plan status, credit balance, tool usage status, and timestamps in our private database. We do not store Google access or refresh tokens.

## Local browser tools

Video conversion, compression, trimming, merging, audio extraction, GIF creation, and local image utilities run in your browser. Files selected for these local tools are not uploaded to our servers.

## Temporary media

Prepared files are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket and delivered through short-lived signed links. Trial objects are scheduled for automatic deletion after 24 hours.

Images submitted to the AI background remover are uploaded privately, processed by our configured AI provider, and retained only as needed to complete and deliver the request. Credit reservations and success or failure records are stored for account accuracy and abuse prevention.

## Service providers

We use Google authentication, Vercel hosting, Google Cloud Run, Neon Postgres, Cloudflare R2, and Replicate to operate the service. These providers may process technical logs or submitted AI-tool inputs needed to provide, secure, and monitor the requested feature.

## Your choices

Submit only media that you own or are authorized to process. Do not submit confidential URLs, unlawful content, or personal data belonging to another person. You may stop using the service at any time; contact details for data requests are provided through the site's published support channel when available.
