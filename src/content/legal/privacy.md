Video Downloader uses Google sign-in to identify your trial account. We store your Google account identifier and email address, the public source URL you submitted, the selected format, job status, and timestamps in our private database.

## Temporary media

Prepared files are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket and delivered through short-lived signed links. Trial objects are scheduled for automatic deletion after 24 hours.

## Service providers

We use Google authentication, Vercel hosting, Google Cloud Run, Neon Postgres, and Cloudflare R2 to operate the service. These providers may process technical logs needed for security and reliability.

## Your choices

Submit only public media that you own or have permission to download. Do not submit confidential URLs or personal data belonging to another person.
