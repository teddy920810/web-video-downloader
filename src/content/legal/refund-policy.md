**Last updated: September 13, 2026**

Paid checkout is not currently available on Streamnest. Selecting a plan or credit pack does not create a charge, subscription, renewal, or paid entitlement. The purchase rules below will apply when payments open.

Streamnest is operated by **Shi Yao**, an individual developer based in mainland China. For billing or refund assistance, contact [support@streamnest.io](mailto:support@streamnest.io).

## Before purchasing

Review the [price, credits and expiration dates](/pricing), renewal terms, and total shown before payment. Current browser-local tools remain free. A purchase is completed only when the payment provider confirms it.

## 7-day refund window

You may request a full refund within **7 calendar days** of each Pro subscription payment, **including renewals**, or a one-time credit pack purchase, provided none of the credits granted by that payment have been consumed. Credits reserved for failed tasks and fully returned do not count as consumed. Use of credits from another payment or a promotional batch does not affect this payment's eligibility. Credits from a refunded payment will be revoked.

We do not normally offer prorated refunds after credits from a payment have been consumed. The exceptions below and any mandatory consumer rights still apply. This voluntary refund window does not limit any statutory withdrawal or refund rights that apply to you.

## Billing errors and service problems

Subject to applicable law and the terms shown at checkout, we may approve a refund or correction when:

- the same order was charged more than once;
- a confirmed cancellation was followed by an incorrect renewal charge;
- credits were purchased but never delivered to the correct Streamnest account;
- a material technical defect prevents the purchased service from working and we cannot provide a reasonable remedy; or
- the law in your location requires a refund.

Email [support@streamnest.io](mailto:support@streamnest.io) with your order reference, account email, and a description of the problem. We will investigate and provide an appropriate remedy. Do not send complete card or bank details.

## Credits and completed services

Background Remover reserves a credit before processing. Successful processing consumes that credit; a provider failure returns the reservation. Returning a credit is not a cash refund. Contact support if a failed task has not returned its credit. Mandatory rights and the remedies for billing errors or service defects remain unaffected.

## Subscriptions and cancellation

When subscriptions become available, you will be able to cancel future renewals from your account. Ordinary cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period and does not automatically refund that period. Monthly credits remain available until that period ends and do not roll over.

If we refund the current subscription payment, that period ends, its credits are revoked, and future renewals are cancelled. Separately purchased credit packs keep their original 24-month expiration dates unless separately refunded.

## How refunds are returned

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment provider and payment method. Bank and payment-network processing times are outside Streamnest's control. Taxes, currency conversion, and provider fees are handled according to applicable law and the payment provider's rules.

## Requesting a refund

Send your account email, order reference, and reason for the request to [support@streamnest.io](mailto:support@streamnest.io). We respond within **3 business days**. This is a response commitment, not a guarantee that funds will arrive within that time. Approved refunds return to the original payment method; posting times depend on the payment provider.
