No downloader truly works the same way on every website. Each platform has its own player, permissions, formats, access controls, and terms. A reliable workflow starts by checking support and ownership before pasting a URL.

## Check whether the video is eligible

Only download content you own or have permission to save. Public visibility alone does not create a right to copy, edit, or republish a video.

Avoid private accounts, paid courses, subscription libraries, rentals, and streams protected by access controls. Use the platform's official offline feature when one exists.

## Confirm the supported platform

The web trial currently supports YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram for eligible public links. Platform support can change, so the list next to the URL field should be treated as authoritative.

Do not spend the one-time trial testing a site that is not listed. Broader workflows are planned for the desktop direction, but unsupported services should remain clearly labeled until they are verified.

## Copy a clean URL

Use the specific video or post page, not a homepage, search result, profile, or embedded frame. Remove surrounding text from copied share messages. You can test whether a link is truly public by opening it in a signed-out browser window.

## Analyze before downloading

Paste the URL and let the service return metadata and available formats. Confirm the preview and title belong to the intended video. The analysis step does not guarantee that every format will remain available, so choose from the current response.

## Choose a compatible result

MP4 is usually the most portable video format. For the free trial, stay within 720p, 10 minutes, and 500 MB. Lower resolutions use less bandwidth and storage and are often sufficient for mobile viewing.

## Protect privacy and accounts

A public-media downloader should not need the password for the source platform. Do not upload browser cookies or session exports to an unfamiliar service. Sign in only to the downloader's own account when required for trial tracking.

## Respond to failures safely

If a link is private, removed, protected, region restricted, or unsupported, stop and use the official platform. If only a listed format fails, analyze once more and select another available option. Avoid endless retries and avoid tools that ask you to disable security controls.

## Keep a responsible archive

Save the creator, source URL, date, and permission with the file. Credit the owner where required and do not redistribute the download beyond the authorized use.
