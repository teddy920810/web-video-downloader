Hulu programs are licensed streaming content. The reliable way to watch supported titles without a connection is the official offline download feature in Hulu's supported apps and plans.

## Use the official offline download

Install the current Hulu app on a supported mobile device, sign in to an eligible plan, and look for the download icon on the title page. Not every movie or episode is downloadable because rights vary by title.

An official offline download stays inside the Hulu app. It is not a general MP4 file and may expire after a period of time or after playback begins. Connect periodically so the app can verify the subscription and refresh eligible downloads.

## Prepare before a trip

1. Update the Hulu app while you have a stable connection.
2. Confirm that the title shows a download control.
3. Choose a quality that fits the free storage on the device.
4. Download early enough to verify playback before leaving Wi-Fi.
5. Pack a charger and, if needed, headphones that work with the device.

## Why a web downloader is not appropriate

Subscription streams may use access controls and DRM. A public-URL analyzer is designed for eligible public media, not for bypassing a streaming service's protections. Do not enter Hulu credentials into an unrelated downloader.

Our web tool should not claim Hulu support, and it will not turn a protected Hulu stream into a normal file. Use the official offline download instead.

## If the download button is missing

Check the plan, device support, app version, region, and the title's availability. If the title is not eligible, there is no safe third-party shortcut. You can add it to your watchlist and stream it when a connection is available.

## Keep offline viewing reliable

Leave enough device storage for app updates, keep the account active, and avoid signing out before your trip. Official downloads are designed for temporary offline access, so maintain a backup viewing plan for long journeys.
