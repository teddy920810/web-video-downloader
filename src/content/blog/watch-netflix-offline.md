Netflix provides offline viewing for eligible titles through supported versions of its official apps. Because the catalog and device rules change, the download icon on the title page is the clearest signal that an item is available.

## Download an eligible title

Open Netflix on a supported device, choose a movie or episode, and select the download icon. For a series, manage episodes from the season list or use any official smart-download option available in the app.

## Balance quality and storage

Standard quality is faster and uses less space. Higher quality can help on a large tablet but may significantly increase file size. Check the app's storage view before downloading a full season.

| Situation | Practical choice |
| --- | --- |
| Short commute | A few episodes at standard quality |
| Long flight | A tested mix of movies and episodes |
| Limited storage | Lower quality and remove watched items |

## Expect time and account limits

Downloaded Netflix titles remain inside the app and may expire. Some require you to reconnect after a set period, and availability can change when a title leaves the catalog. An active account and periodic internet access are part of the official offline experience.

## Do not use a public-link downloader

Netflix video is subscription content protected by access controls. A tool designed for eligible public URLs should not request Netflix credentials or promise to remove those controls.

If a download is unavailable, there is no safe setting in this site's web trial that will make a protected Netflix title downloadable. Use the official offline download or stream later.

## Troubleshooting checklist

Update the app, restart the device, verify available storage, check the plan and device limit, and reconnect to refresh the account. If only one title is affected, its licensing terms may not allow downloads.
